## Depth Anything V2 Small (Core ML)

Matrix Eyes Cam bundles Apple's Core ML conversion of Depth Anything V2 Small
(`DepthAnythingV2SmallF16`), used unmodified for on-device depth estimation.

- Source: https://huggingface.co/apple/coreml-depth-anything-v2-small
- Original model: Depth Anything V2, Lihe Yang et al., https://github.com/DepthAnything/Depth-Anything-V2
- License: Apache License 2.0, https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

The model is not part of this repository's source; `scripts/fetch-model.sh` downloads it.
